3. Who we share it with

We share data only with service providers that help run PlotLine, only as needed for them to do so:

  • Amazon Web Services: stores app data.
  • Fly.io: hosts our servers.
  • Plaid: connects your bank accounts if you choose to link them. See Plaid's End User Privacy Policy.
  • OpenAI: processes content for AI features, such as receipt images, food photos, budget and portfolio inputs, and meal ideas.
  • Twilio: sends verification and password reset text messages.
  • Apple and Google: Sign in with Apple, Google Sign-In, Google Calendar import (if you choose it), and App Store payments.
  • Food and market data services (USDA FoodData Central, Open Food Facts, Financial Modeling Prep, NewsAPI): receive only the search terms, barcodes or stock symbols you look up, not your account information.

Other people in PlotLine: your username, profile photo and trophies can be seen by other PlotLine users. Your name, birthday and city are shown only to your friends. Posts, chat messages, and calendars or lists you share are visible to the friends you share them with. Profile photos are stored at a web address that anyone with the link can open.

We may also share information if required by law, to protect someone's safety, or as part of a merger or sale of the business (in which case this policy would continue to apply). We do not sell your personal information or share it for advertising.

4. How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account exists. When you delete your account (Profile → Delete Account), we delete your account and associated data, disconnect linked banks through Plaid, revoke Sign in with Apple access, and remove you from friends' lists, shared lists and the feed. Copies in backups or logs may take up to 30 days to disappear.

5. Security

We use encryption in transit (HTTPS), hash passwords, require a signed-in session for account data, and limit each account to its own data. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password and keep your device secure.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Access and correct: view and edit most information in the app, or email us.
  • Delete: delete your account in the app at any time.
  • Permissions: camera, photos, location and notifications are optional and can be turned off in iOS Settings.
  • Text messages: reply STOP to opt out.

Depending on where you live (for example California), you may have additional rights, such as to know what we collect or to request a copy. Email [CONTACT EMAIL] and we'll respond within the time the law requires. We won't treat you differently for using these rights.
